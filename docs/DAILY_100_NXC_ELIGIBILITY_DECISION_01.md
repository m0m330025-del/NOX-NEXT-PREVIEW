# Daily 100 NXC Eligibility Decision 01

- Status: Provisional product decision; documentation only.
- Decision: The daily 100 NXC claim is available to all users, including men and women.
- Claim flow: Eligible users see the claim popup when opening the app and must press «استلام».
- Cooldown: One successful claim per rolling 24-hour period.
- Enforcement: Eligibility and cooldown must be checked against authoritative server state with idempotency protection.
- Scope: NXC is an internal conceptual app unit only. This decision does not authorize live issuance, wallet operations, or backend changes.
