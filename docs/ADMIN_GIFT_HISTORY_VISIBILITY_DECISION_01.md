# Administration Gift History Visibility — Decision 01

**Status:** Provisionally approved; documentation only; not implemented or activated.

## Decision
An administration gift remains visible in the recipient's profile gift history. The recipient cannot hide or remove the gift-history entry.

## Separation of records
- This rule concerns the gift-history entry, not the optional profile post.
- The recipient may still delete the separately shared profile post under the previously approved rule.
- Deleting a profile post does not remove the gift-history entry, wallet activity, immutable administration note, or restricted audit record.
- The gift-history entry is read-only.

## Activation boundary
Documentation only. No Appwrite schema, function, workflow, secret, or runtime changes; no wallet or gift operation is activated. NXC remains an internal conceptual unit, not cash or real-world monetary value.
