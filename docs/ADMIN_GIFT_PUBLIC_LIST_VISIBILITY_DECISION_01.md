# Administration Gift Public-List Visibility — Decision 01

**Status:** Provisionally approved; documentation only; not implemented or activated.

## Decision
Administration gifts do not appear in any public gift list or public activity feed. They are visible only to the recipient and authorized administration users.

## Visibility rules
- The recipient can view the gift in their own profile gift history and wallet activity.
- Authorized administration users can view the relevant administration gift records according to their permissions.
- Other members cannot browse or discover another member's administration gifts through public lists or feeds.
- The recipient's optional profile-sharing action remains separate and voluntary, subject to the previously approved one-time sharing rule.
- The underlying gift history remains immutable and visible to the recipient.

## Activation boundary
Documentation only. No Appwrite schema, function, workflow, secret, or runtime changes; no wallet or gift operation is activated. NXC remains an internal conceptual unit, not cash or real-world monetary value.
