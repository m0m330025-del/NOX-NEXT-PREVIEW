# Registration WhatsApp Contact Handling Decision 01

- Status: Provisional product decision; documentation only.
- Clarification: NOX NEXT will not display, store, or manage a WhatsApp phone number for this process. The project owner/admin will contact the applicant personally using their own known contact method.
- Applicant flow: The applicant submits a registration request in NOX NEXT. The owner/admin contacts the applicant outside the app and explains the age-verification process.
- Review flow: The owner/admin reviews the evidence outside NOX NEXT and records only the outcome, reviewer identifier, timestamp, and minimal reason code in the app.
- Activation: The account remains pending until the 18+ requirement has been verified and the authorized reviewer records approval.
- Privacy boundary: Do not place ID images or full ID numbers in NOX NEXT, logs, analytics, exports, or ordinary admin records. Limit access to evidence outside the app and delete it promptly under a documented, legally reviewed retention policy.
- Scope: No WhatsApp integration, phone-number configuration, automated messaging, identity collection, Appwrite change, or deployment is authorized.
