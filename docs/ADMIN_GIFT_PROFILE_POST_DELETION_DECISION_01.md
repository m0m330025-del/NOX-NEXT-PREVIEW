# Shared Administration Gift Profile Post Deletion — Decision 01

**Status:** Provisionally approved; documentation only; not implemented or activated.

## Decision
A member who voluntarily publishes an administration-gift post on their NOX profile may delete that profile post at any time.

## Separation of records
- Deleting the profile post removes only the public/member-facing post.
- It does not cancel, reverse, or alter the completed gift.
- It does not delete the immutable gift-history entry, original administration note, or restricted internal audit record.
- The member may not use post deletion to alter wallet balances or gift status.

## Activation boundary
Documentation only. No Appwrite schema, function, workflow, secret, or runtime changes; no wallet or gift operation is activated. NXC remains an internal conceptual unit, not cash or real-world monetary value.
