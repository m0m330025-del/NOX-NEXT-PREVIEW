# Administration Gift Profile Resharing — Decision 01

**Status:** Provisionally approved; documentation only; not implemented or activated.

## Decision
A recipient may publish a given administration-gift profile post only once. If the recipient deletes that post, the same gift cannot be shared again.

## Separation of records
- Deleting the post does not cancel, reverse, or alter the completed gift.
- The immutable gift history, original administration note, and restricted audit record remain intact.
- The one-time sharing restriction applies to the profile post for that specific gift; it does not restrict sharing unrelated content.

## Activation boundary
Documentation only. No Appwrite schema, function, workflow, secret, or runtime changes; no wallet or gift operation is activated. NXC remains an internal conceptual unit, not cash or real-world monetary value.
