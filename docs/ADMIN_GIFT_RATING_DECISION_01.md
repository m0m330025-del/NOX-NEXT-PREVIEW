# Administration Gift Ratings and Comments — Decision 01

**Status:** Provisionally approved; documentation only; not implemented or activated.

## Decision
Recipients cannot rate administration gifts or add comments/reviews to the gift record.

## Boundaries
- No rating stars, score, review, or comment field is provided for administration gifts.
- Existing independent messaging features, if any, are unaffected.
- Gift history remains read-only and permanently visible to the recipient.
- The immutable administration note and restricted audit record remain unchanged.

## Activation boundary
Documentation only. No Appwrite schema, function, workflow, secret, or runtime changes; no wallet or gift operation is activated. NXC remains an internal conceptual unit, not cash or real-world monetary value.
