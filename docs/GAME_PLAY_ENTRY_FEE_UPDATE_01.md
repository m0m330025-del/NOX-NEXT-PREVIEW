# Game Entry Fee Update — Decision 01

**Status:** Provisionally approved; documentation only; not implemented or activated.

## Decision
The game entry fee is updated from 5 to **10 coins (NXC)** per game entry.

## Unchanged play rewards
- 100 NXC for each completed 30-minute eligible play interval.
- Maximum 200 NXC per eligible hour.

## Other rules
- The entry fee is charged once per game entry.
- Play rewards require completed, server-verified eligible play intervals.
- Entry charges and play rewards are separate auditable events.
- The exact treatment of interrupted sessions and carry-over of partial time remains undecided.

## Activation boundary
Documentation only. No Appwrite schema, function, workflow, secret, runtime change, or live wallet/game operation is performed or activated. NXC is an internal conceptual app unit, not cash or real-world monetary value.
