# Administration Gift Eligibility — Decision 01

**Status:** Provisionally approved; documentation only; not implemented or activated.

## Decision
Administration-issued NXC gifts may be directed to both men and women, subject to the same eligibility checks and applicable authorization rules.

This does not change the previously established restriction that men cannot receive member-to-member gifts; administration gifts are a separate event type.

## Boundaries
- NXC remains a conceptual internal app unit, not cash or real-world monetary value.
- This decision does not enable real-money top-ups, cash-outs, payouts, or member cash exchange.
- No live issuance, balance editing, or wallet operation is enabled.
- Documentation only: no Appwrite schema, function, workflow, secret, or runtime changes.
