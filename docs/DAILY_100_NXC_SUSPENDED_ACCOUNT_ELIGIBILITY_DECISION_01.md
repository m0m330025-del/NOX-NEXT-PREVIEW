# Daily 100 NXC Suspended Account Eligibility Decision 01

- Status: Provisional product decision; documentation only.
- Decision: Accounts that are suspended are not eligible to claim the daily 100 NXC reward for the duration of the suspension.
- After suspension ends: Eligibility may resume according to the normal rolling 24-hour claim rule, based on the last successful claim and authoritative server state. Suspension does not itself grant a missed or backdated claim.
- Enforcement: The server must verify account status at claim time and record denied attempts without issuing NXC.
- Scope: NXC remains an internal conceptual app unit only. This decision does not authorize live issuance, wallet operations, or backend changes.
